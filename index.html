My Day, My Routine

What do you do every day?

Audience: teenagers and adults starting English.

Learning outcome: I can talk and write about my daily routine using the present simple and times of the day.

You will learn new expressions, listen to Anna, read about Tom, practice with activities and take a final quiz.

Your learning path

Summary: a day in time

Grammar: Present simple

We use the present simple for things we do every day. With he / she / it we add -s (or -es: go → goes, brush → brushes). We use at with times: at 7:00.

SubjectExample
I / you / we / theyI wake up at 6:30.
he / she / itShe wakes up at 6:30.
NegativeHe doesn't have lunch at home.

A Day with Tom

Before reading: Look at the pictures. What do you think Tom does in the morning?

Tom is a student. He wakes up at 7:00 and has breakfast with his family. At 8:00 he goes to school by bike. In the afternoon, he does his homework and plays video games. He goes to bed at 9:30 because he gets up early.

Glossary: student = a person who studies · by bike = on a bicycle · early = before the usual time · homework = school work at home

While and after reading

Compare: Is your day similar to Tom's? Write one sentence: “I … but Tom …”.

1. Recognition: what is the action?

2. Application: choose the correct form

3. Use in context: real situations

Write it!

Write 5 sentences about your own routine with times.

Criteria: 5 sentences · times with “at” · -s with he/she · capital letters and periods
